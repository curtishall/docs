# Sandbox Realism

Cadmus provides a server-driven simulation environment for staff training, dispatcher drills, and demonstration exercises known as **Sandbox Realism**.

Unlike simulated or "fake" CAD environments that inject artificial local alerts into the user interface, Sandbox Realism feeds generated traffic through the **same real CAD ingestion pipelines, GIS resolver, and WebSocket event streams used in production**.

<Note>
Sandbox Realism is only available in organizations designated as **Sandbox**. Live production organizations cannot start simulations or generate simulated events.
</Note>

## Opening the Sandbox Control Center

1. Look at the top navigation bar. When logged into a Sandbox organization, the top header displays the orange **SANDBOX MODE** badge.
2. Hovering over the Cadmus Dispatch logo continues to display the software version number without shifting surrounding navigation controls.
3. Click the **SANDBOX MODE** badge to open the **Sandbox Control Center** side panel.

## Realism Levels

The Control Center offers preset realism speeds:

* **Off**: No unsolicited simulation events are generated.
* **Quiet**: Suitable for low-stress exploration (events generated roughly every 15–25 minutes).
* **Normal** *(Default)*: Typical daytime call pace (events generated roughly every 6–12 minutes).
* **Busy**: High-volume training pace for multi-dispatcher training exercises.
* **Manual**: Automated background scheduling is paused; dispatchers trigger events manually using the **Generate Event Now** section.

## What is Simulated vs Real

* **Real GIS Resolution**: Calls are placed at real address points, road centerlines, and parcels within your agency's actual coverage jurisdiction.
* **Real CAD Pipeline**: Generated calls create authentic pending incidents via the production dispatch service and broadcast in real time to all connected dispatchers.
* **Multi-User Synchronized**: When a supervisor starts Realism, all dispatchers and MDT users logged into that Sandbox organization see the simulated calls and status updates simultaneously.
* **Zero Production Impact**: Simulations cannot cross into Live organizations, and credentials for production 911 telco, RapidSOS, SMS providers, or CJIS networks are never invoked.

## Generating Events Manually

Supervisors and trainers can generate specific incidents on demand:

* **Generate Law Call**: Dispatches a realistic law enforcement call at a sampled jurisdiction location.
* **Generate Fire Call**: Creates a priority structure fire incident.
* **Generate EMS Call**: Generates a high-priority medical emergency call.
