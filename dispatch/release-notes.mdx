## August 2026 — Call location validation

- **Validate and Geocode** on call detail now uses **address fields only** — Call Description / complaint narrative is never sent to geocoding.
- Addresses resolved **outside your organization's immediate coverage** show a warning and are **not auto-saved** to the CFS location unless you explicitly confirm outside coverage.
- Changing unit status or opening call detail no longer triggers background geocoding of complaint text.

If a map pin jumps unexpectedly after this update, use **Validate and Geocode Address** on the correct street address to restore the local pin.
