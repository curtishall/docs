Web Admin **Call History** searches your organization's full archive of closed calls. It is not limited to the most recent calls shown on a dispatch board.

## Who can use it

- **Dispatchers** and **organization administrators** can open **Call History** from the Web Admin sidebar.
- Agency-scoped users see results for agencies they can access.

## Open Call History

1. Sign in to **Web Admin**.
2. In the sidebar, click **Call History**.

## Search filters

Combine any filters below. Every filter you fill must match (AND).

| Filter | What it matches |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Call type** | Exact incident type from your organization's type list. Retired types remain available for older calls. |
| **Officer / unit** | Officer name, badge or DSN, or unit call sign from historical assignment records. |
| **Quick search** | Description, notes, and other narrative text. |
| **CFS #** | Agency CFS number fragment or full number. |
| **Address** | Street, place name, or location fragment. |
| **Date range** | Closed-call date window. |
| **Report Taken only** | Limits results to calls flagged report-to-follow. |
| **Advanced search** | Name, SSN, DOB, license plate, VIN, and driver license. |

<Tip>
  Start with **Call type** and **Officer / unit** when you know the nature of the call and who responded. Add a date range if the list is still large.
</Tip>

## Results and detail

- Results are paginated. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move through pages.
- Click a row to open call detail, review narratives and notes, and download a PDF when your role allows.

## Historical officer matching

Calls closed **after** this release store durable officer and unit snapshots at assignment time. Older calls may still match primary officer or unit data, but secondary officers who rode a unit are best-effort for legacy records.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| No results for a known officer | Search by call sign or DSN instead of a nickname. |
| Call type missing from dropdown | The type may be retired; choose the retired entry or search without a type filter. |
| Too many matches | Narrow with date range, CFS number, or advanced person/vehicle fields. |

## Release note

**August 2026** — Call History now supports **Call type** and **Officer / unit** filters on full-archive search.
