**Web Admin → Fuel Logs** lets supervisors and administrators review fuel fills recorded from Dispatch CAD.

## Who can use this page

| Action | Typical roles |
|---|---|
| Search, view, export | OrgAdmin (all agencies in the organization), AgencyAdmin, AgencySupervisor, Dispatcher, CadmusAdmin |
| Edit or delete a log | OrgAdmin, AgencyAdmin, AgencySupervisor, CadmusAdmin |

Dispatchers normally **create** logs in Dispatch CAD. Use Web Admin to audit, correct, or export.

OrgAdmins see fuel logs for **every agency** in their organization (not only their primary agency). Use the agency filter when you want one agency’s rows.

## Search and export

Filter by vehicle number, unit number, agency (when you manage multiple agencies), fuel type, and date range. Use **Export CSV** to download matching rows, including beginning pump, ending pump, and entered-by DSN.

## What each entry shows

- **Date/Time** — when the fill was saved
- **Vehicle / Unit** — numbers captured on the log
- **Gallons** — amount entered by the dispatcher
- **Beginning / Ending pump** — optional station meter readings used to verify gallons mathematically
- **Entered By** — dispatcher name, with **DSN** when it was on their user profile at save time
- **Odometer, fuel type, location, notes** — optional details

## Editing a log

Open an entry and choose **Edit** (when your role allows). You can correct gallons, pump readings, odometer, fuel type, location, and notes. Ending pump must be greater than or equal to beginning pump when both are set. Clearing a pump field removes that reading.

## Change history

Opening a fuel log shows a **Change history** section. Creates, edits, and deletes are listed with who made the change and when. Edits include field-level before → after values (for example gallons or pump readings).

## Vehicle detail

Recent fuel fills also appear on a vehicle’s detail view, including pump range and entered-by DSN when present.
