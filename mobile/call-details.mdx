## Status buttons (on assigned call)

When your unit is on the call:

| Button | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **DISPATCHED** | Acknowledge assignment |
| **EN ROUTE** | En route to scene |
| **ON SCENE** | Arrived on scene |
| **CLEAR CALL** | Clear your unit from call |

Requires network — offline shows **No network connection. Try again when online.**

## Call sections

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **CALL TYPE** | Incident type and priority |
| **PRIMARY UNIT** | Primary assigned unit |
| **LOCATION** | Address, city, and prior calls at this address |
| **REPORTING PARTY** | Caller info and prior calls from this caller |
| **PERSONS INVOLVED** | Suspects, victims, witnesses |
| **VEHICLES INVOLVED** | Vehicle records |
| **History & Logs** | Notes and audit trail |

### Location

The **LOCATION** card shows the incident address and city. Use **OPEN MAP** to jump to the map. Expand **Calls at this address** to review prior calls at the same location from the last 90 days, then tap a row to open that call.

### Reporting party

The **REPORTING PARTY** card shows the caller name, phone, and address when available. When a phone number is present, expand **Calls from this caller** to review prior calls from that phone number (last 90 days). Tap a row to open that call.
