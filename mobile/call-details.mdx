## Status buttons (on assigned call)

When the call is not yet **On Scene**:

| Button | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **EN ROUTE** | Your unit is en route to the scene |
| **ON SCENE** | Your unit has arrived |
| **CLEAR CALL** | Clear yourself or close the call (see [Close call](/mobile/close-call)) |
| **UNITS** | Open the units sheet |

When the **call** is already **On Scene**, **EN ROUTE** and **ON SCENE** hide on the action bar so you do not restamp a scene that is already occupied. Use **UNITS** if you are a late unit and still need to mark your own apparatus En Route or On Scene.

Requires network — offline shows **No network connection. Try again when online.**

## UNITS sheet

**UNITS** always opens a roster of who is on the call (call sign and status).

- If you are not assigned yet, Cadmus assigns your apparatus first, then opens the sheet (it does not stop at a “Unit assigned to call” toast).
- **Your** unit can tap **EN ROUTE**, **ON SCENE**, or **CLEAR MYSELF** based on *your* unit’s status — even when another unit is already On Scene.
- Other units on the roster are read-only.

## Call sections

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **CALL TYPE** | Incident type and priority |
| **PRIMARY UNIT** | Primary assigned unit |
| **REPORTING PARTY** | Caller info |
| **PERSONS INVOLVED** | Suspects, victims, witnesses |
| **VEHICLES INVOLVED** | Vehicle records |
| **History & Logs** | Notes and audit trail |
