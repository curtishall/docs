Close a call from the field MDT when authorized.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: Close call dialog on mobile</Note>

## Open dialog

From call detail, tap **CLEAR CALL**.

### Other units attached

If other units are still on the call, Cadmus asks:

> Other units are attached to this call, clear yourself or clear and close the call?

| Choice | What happens |
|--------|----------------|
| **Clear myself** | Removes **your** unit only. The call stays open for the remaining units. Success toast **Cleared from call**. |
| **Clear and close the call** | Opens the close-call dialog and closes the whole incident for everyone. |
| **Cancel** | No change. |

If you are the **last** unit, Cadmus skips that choice and opens the close dialog. **Clear myself** is refused when you are last — close the call instead.

## Fields

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Public narrative** | Incident summary for record |
| **Private notes** | Restricted notes when supported |
| **Report to follow (RTF)** | Flag for records follow-up |

## After the call is closed

From **Find a Call**, tap **Add/Edit CFS notes** on a result to update public narrative and private notes.

Supervisors and administrators see **Supervisor approved CFS** — check when narratives are reviewed and approved for release.

## Submit

- **Close call** button
- Success: **Call closed** snackbar

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: RTF checkbox and Close call button</Note>

## Tips

- Match agency SOP for minimum narrative length.
- Use RTF when report number will be assigned later.
- Confirm with dispatch before choosing **Clear and close the call** on a multi-unit incident if you are not primary.

## Warnings

<Warning>Closing may lock notes and evidence uploads — confirm scene is complete.</Warning>

<Warning>Requires network — cannot close offline.</Warning>
