A field interview report (FIR) records a voluntary contact: who you spoke with, where, and
what was said. Reports are created on the MDT and reviewed from the same app.

## Opening the report list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open it from the dashboard">
    On the **Dashboard**, tap the **FIELD INTERVIEW** chip. It opens the report list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or open it from NCIC">
    From **NCIC Search**, tap **CREATE FIR** to reach the same report list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The dashboard chip used to be labeled **CRASH REPORT**. There is no crash-report form — the
  chip and the **NCIC** **CREATE FIR** button both open field interview reports.
</Note>

## Reviewing reports

The list is titled **FIELD INTERVIEW REPORTS** and shows one row per report with its date,
location, subject, and status.

- **Search** — type in **SEARCH REPORTS...** to filter by subject, location, or narrative.
- **Status filter** — **All**, **Draft**, **Submitted**, or **Approved**.
- **Open a row** to see the full narrative, then **FIR DETAILS** to review it.
- **Delete** a draft to remove it. Deletion is recorded in the audit log.

## Writing a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new report">
    Tap **NEW FIR**. The **CREATE FIELD INTERVIEW REPORT** form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the report">
    Record the location, the subject's name and date of birth, the incident type, and the
    narrative of what was said.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the report. It is stored as a **Draft** until it is submitted for approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Report statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Saved on the device/server but not yet submitted. |
| **Submitted** | Sent for supervisor review. |
| **Approved** | Reviewed and accepted. |

## Tips

- Write the narrative while the details are fresh.
- Include the exact location you used, not the call's address, if the interview moved.
- Save a **Draft** even if the interview is cut short — you can finish it later.

<Warning>
  A field interview report documents a voluntary contact. Follow agency policy on consent,
  recording, and disclosure before you file one.
</Warning>
