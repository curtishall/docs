View and manage calls assigned to you or your agency.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: Calls screen with MY CALLS, ALL CALLS, and RECENT tabs</Note>

## Navigation

Sidebar **Calls** → **CALLS FOR SERVICE**

## Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|-----|---------|
| **MY CALLS** | Calls your unit is assigned to |
| **ALL CALLS** | Open agency calls only (see below) |
| **RECENT** | Newest closed calls (see below) |

### ALL CALLS — open calls only

**ALL CALLS** lists calls that are still active on the board. Statuses shown:

- **PENDING**, **DISPATCHED**, **ACTIVE**, **EN ROUTE**, **ON SCENE**

**Closed** and **Cancelled** calls do not appear on **ALL CALLS**. When a call closes, it drops off this tab and may appear on **RECENT**.

### RECENT — recently closed

**RECENT** shows the **150 most recently closed** calls for your agency. There is no **Load more** — the list is fixed at those newest closes.

The **RECENT** list loads the first time you open that tab. It is not loaded when the app starts.

For older closed calls beyond those 150, use sidebar **Find a Call** (see [Find a call (mobile)](/mobile/find-a-call)).

## Call card badges

Status labels on list rows:

- **PENDING**, **DISPATCHED**, **ACTIVE**, **EN ROUTE**, **ON SCENE**
- **ProQA**, **ProQA ALERT**, **RTF** (report to follow) when applicable

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: Call list row with status badges</Note>

## Empty and load states

- **No active calls.** when nothing matches on **MY CALLS** or **ALL CALLS**.
- **No recently closed calls.** when **RECENT** has no closed calls.

## Open a call

- Tap a row from **MY CALLS**, **ALL CALLS**, or **RECENT** to open call detail.
- The app loads full call detail when you open a row. A loading indicator appears while detail loads; if the first attempt fails, the app retries automatically.
- Full screen on phone; split panel on desktop/tablet.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: Call detail split view on tablet/desktop</Note>

## Pull to refresh

Pull down on **MY CALLS** or **ALL CALLS** to reload open calls. **RECENT** reloads when you open the tab or pull to refresh on that tab.

## Tips

- Use **MY CALLS** during patrol for your assignments only.
- **ALL CALLS** for situational awareness when policy allows — open calls only.
- **RECENT** for today's or this week's closes without searching the full archive.
- If **ALL CALLS** is empty after a known dispatch, check unit/call sign in profile.
- For a CFS number older than the **RECENT** list, use **Find a Call**.

## Warnings

<Warning>Demo/fallback data should not appear in production — if you see unexpected calls, report to dispatch.</Warning>
