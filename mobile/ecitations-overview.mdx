Electronic citations and warnings from the MDT.

## MDT beta

- Top bar shows **BETA**. Production installs stay on the production update channel.
- Click **SANDBOX** / **LIVE** in the top bar to switch paired organization spaces.
- Draft and queued citations can be resumed. Signatures are not captured yet.

## New citation wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose citation or warning">
    Tap **NEW CITATION** and toggle **CITATION** vs **WARNING**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete wizard steps">
    Fill **Subject / Vehicle**, **Violation / Loc**, **Signatures**, and **Review & Issue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue or save draft">
    Tap **ISSUE CITATION** or **SAVE DRAFT**. Signatures are **not captured** in this beta — review the printed form with the violator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resume, edit, and delete

- **RESUME** / **EDIT** reopen **draft** and **PENDING SYNC** citations in the wizard. The same ticket identity is kept so sync updates the same record.
- Queued (**PENDING SYNC**) citations stay queued after you save edits. Issue or retry sync when the network is back.
- **VIEW** / **PRINT** apply to submitted, printed, and accepted citations. Those cannot be edited.
- **DELETE** is available for **drafts only**, with confirmation. Submitted history cannot be deleted from the MDT.

## Print

- Open **PDF PREVIEW** from a citation.
- **PRINT** is available only after the citation is synced (submitted, printed, or accepted). Draft and queued citations show **EDIT** instead.
- When your agency uses a **state or custom form template**, choose:
  - **4" THERMAL** — single-strip layout for mobile 4″ printers (Missouri: page 1 and 2 sequential)
  - **LETTER (FRONT + BACK)** — letter-size page with front and back side-by-side
- If the agency template is not cached, Missouri agencies can print a clearly labeled **built-in state form**. Other states are blocked until the template downloads — Cadmus will not silently use a generic form.
- Use **PRINT** from the preview once the form shows correctly (not a blank page).
- **BACK TO E-CITATIONS** returns to the citation list.

## Offline and sync

- Drafts and queued citations stay visible on the device if citation history cannot load. A notice explains that you are seeing local copies.
- **Auto-Sync Ticket Data** in Settings uploads when online.
- Sync failures report how many queued citations failed, without raw server traces.
- See [Connectivity and offline](/mobile/connectivity-offline).

## Requirements

- Agency **Traffic Stops Allowed** in Web Admin.
- Bluetooth printer paired if printing in field.

## Warnings

<Warning>Issued citations are legal documents — verify identity and statute before **ISSUE CITATION**.</Warning>

<Warning>**PENDING SYNC** means records may not have citation yet — confirm sync before leaving stop.</Warning>
